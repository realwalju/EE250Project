# Demo video plan (4 minutes max, narrated)

Record the screen (OBS) and the desk (phone on a stand) at the same time, then
cut between them. Narrate over the edit afterward; it's easier than talking live.

| Time | Shot | What to say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:20 | Desk wide shot: PC, Pi with e-ink, sensor PCB, mood light | Who we are and the one-line idea: a desk that reacts to your Fortnite games |
| 0:20-0:55 | The block diagram from the write-up | The nodes, MQTT through the Pi, what each one does |
| 0:55-1:50 | Split screen: gameplay + mood light | Get an elimination and a knock; point out the light reacting within a second. Explain template matching and the 3-frame confirmation |
| 1:50-2:25 | Knocked, then slap the desk | Tilt detection: knock + vibration burst, neither alone. Orange light and the companion's reaction |
| 2:25-3:05 | End of a match, then the e-ink close-up | The LLM recap appearing; the status LED double-blinking; press the button to acknowledge |
| 3:05-3:35 | Laptop dashboard | Live event log, room temperature and light, match history |
| 3:35-4:00 | Desk wide shot | One limitation and what we'd improve; thanks |

Tips:
- Do a full dry run first and check every shot is in focus and the e-ink is readable on camera.
- If a win is hard to get on camera, record a few sessions and use the best moments.
- Keep the video public (unlisted is fine) on YouTube and check the link works logged out.
